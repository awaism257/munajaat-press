Little Muslims · ages 2 to 5

My First Islamic Words Colouring Book

40 Simple Words in Arabic & English · Little Muslims, Book 1

Help your little one learn their very first Islamic words, one big picture at a time: Bismillah, Assalamu alaykum, Masjid, Ramadan, Eid and many more. Each word has its own page with the word in clear Arabic script, how to say it and what it means, and a big, simple picture to colour on the opposite page.

  • Large Arabic letters with vowel marks, plus easy English sounds and meanings
  • Big pictures with thick lines for little hands, a fun line to read aloud and a short tip for grown-ups
  • Printed on one side only, so markers don’t spoil the next picture. The name of Allah and verses of the Qur’an are never given as outlines to colour
