The Garden of Purity, Volume 1

The Dawn of Prophethood (Annotated Modernised Edition)

The life of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ as told by the fifteenth-century Persian historian Mīrkhwānd in his Rawḍat al-Ṣafā, in clear modern British English. This volume follows the story from the Prophet's ancestry and the Year of the Elephant, through the years in Makkah and the Hijrah, to the battles of Badr and Uḥud.

“An exquisite work of Islamic historical writing… The modern edition’s annotations are a welcome gift to readers. Very highly recommended.”Asher Syed, Readers’ Favorite (5 stars)
  • Based on Edward Rehatsek’s 1893 translation, rendered into plain modern English
  • Several hundred editorial footnotes that identify people and places and note where the account differs from the early sources

The Madinan Years (Annotated Modernised Edition)

Volume 2 takes up the story after the Battle of Uḥud: the Battle of the Trench, the Treaty of Ḥudaybiyyah, Khaybar, the conquest of Makkah, Ḥunayn and Tabūk, the Year of Deputations and the Farewell Pilgrimage, to the passing of the Prophet ﷺ. The Shield of Al-Aqsa, Volume 1

Born in Exile

Tikrit, 1137. A family flees in the night, and a son is born on the road. Yūsuf ibn Ayyūb grows up in the shadow of greater men: his patient father Ayyūb, his fierce uncle Shīrkūh, and Nūr ad-Dīn Zangī, the just ruler who meant to unite the Muslim world and free Jerusalem. From the walls of Damascus he watches the Second Crusade break in four days, until a desperate exile arrives from Egypt and he is ordered onto a road he never wished to take. History would remember him as Ṣalāḥ ad-Dīn, Salahuddin.

  • Drawn from the chronicles of Ibn Shaddād, Ibn al-Athīr and Abū Shāma; dialogue and some scenes are dramatised, and the book says so
  • A map, timeline, Who’s Who, historical notes, glossary and list of sources at the back

About

About Munajaat Press

Munajaat Press is a small independent publisher of Islamic books in clear British English. It brings classical works on the life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ to modern readers, with honest notes on the sources, and makes illustrated books for Muslim children that never depict people.

Our editions say plainly what has been done to them. The classical texts are based on public-domain translations, newly rendered into modern English with editorial notes, and prepared with AI assistance and human review. The manga and children’s artwork is created with AI tools and checked by hand.
