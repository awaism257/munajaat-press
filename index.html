About

About Munajaat Press

Munajaat Press is a small independent publisher of Islamic books in clear British English. It brings classical Islamic history, from the stories of the prophets to the life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the Rightly Guided Caliphs, to modern readers, with honest notes on the sources, and makes illustrated books for Muslim children that never depict people.

Our editions say plainly what has been done to them. The classical texts are based on public-domain translations, newly rendered into modern English with editorial notes, and prepared with AI assistance and human review. The manga and children’s artwork is created with AI tools and checked by hand.
