Heroes of Islam Novels

Novels about the great figures of Islamic history for readers aged 12 and up, told from the medieval Arabic chronicles. The first series, The Salahuddin Chronicles, follows the life of Salahuddin in six books.

The Salahuddin Chronicles, Book One

The Long Road from Tikrit

Tikrit, 1132. On the banks of the Tigris, a castle keeper helps a defeated army across the river by night, and changes his family’s fate for ever. Years later the family is driven out into the dark, and on the very night they leave, a son is born. They call him Yusuf.

He is ten when the Crusaders come. A great Frankish army camps in the orchards outside Damascus, and from inside the city Yusuf watches it fight for its life. He grows up among soldiers and scholars, in the shadow of his fierce uncle Shirkuh and the just ruler Nur ad-Din, until a stranger arrives from Egypt and Yusuf must ride with his uncle down a road he never wanted to take. History would remember him as Salahuddin.

  • Every chapter follows the medieval Arabic chronicles: Ibn Shaddad, who served Salahuddin, Ibn al-Athir, Ibn al-Qalanisi and others
  • A map, a family tree, The People in This Book, a timeline and a glossary, and The History Behind the Story: what the old books record, and what has been imagined
  • Battles told without gore, and no words or scenes invented for the Prophet ﷺ or his Companions
